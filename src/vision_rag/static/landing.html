Behind the workspace

A clear path from
document to evidence.

Durable local storage and bounded retrieval support the conversation. Gemini generates answers from retrieved evidence; Find pages works without a generation-provider call.

  1. 01

    Add a PDF

    Save an immutable original and queue durable processing.

  2. 02

    Prepare search

    Extract text, render visual pages and build an eligible index.

  3. 03

    Ask your sources

    Search an explicit selection of up to six documents.

  4. 04

    Inspect & keep

    Check cited pages, compare reports and save findings.

A checked page reference identifies the cited evidence. It does not independently verify that an answer is correct.
